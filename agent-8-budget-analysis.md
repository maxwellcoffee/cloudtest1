## 1. Budget Philosophy & Assumptions <a name="assumptions"></a>

**Target builder:** High school student, budget-conscious, first major woodworking project.

**Key assumptions:**
- US pricing, 2025-2026 market rates
- Western red cedar as primary wood (hinoki is priced separately as an upgrade)
- Student labor at $15-20/hr (or self-build at $0/hr if the student IS the builder)
- Basic residential plumbing (no gas lines)
- Municipal water at ~$0.005/gallon (US average)
- Electricity at ~$0.16/kWh (US average)
- Tools assumed NOT already owned (worst case); many can be borrowed or bought used

**Tub dimensions used for material estimates:**
- 2-person: ~36"L x 30"W x 24"H (interior), approx 90-110 gallons
- 4-person: ~60"L x 36"W x 24"H (interior), approx 180-220 gallons

## 4. Monthly Operating Budget <a name="monthly-operating"></a>

## Summary for Decision-Making

**If budget is under $1,500:** Build a 2-person tub with cedar, 120V heater, salt-only treatment, borrow tools. Expect a scrappy but rewarding build.

**If budget is $2,000-3,000:** Build a 2-person tub properly with a 240V heater, UV sanitizer, and quality components. This is the sweet spot for value.

**If budget is $3,000-4,500:** Build a 4-person tub with good components, or a premium 2-person tub with all the extras.

**If budget is $4,500+:** A fully-equipped 4-person tub with professional electrical work and quality throughout.

**Monthly operating cost will be $30-75/month regardless of tub size or treatment method.** The biggest variable is electricity for heating -- a well-insulated cover and daily use pattern matter far more than which sanitization method you choose.
