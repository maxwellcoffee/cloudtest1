Breathing Dark

Close your eyes. After a moment, dark noise rises around you. Its filter opens as you breathe in and settles as you breathe out. Open your eyes and it falls away.

The bar is the eye-closure signal from the face model. The faint band shows its range over the last 15 s.

To set it by hand: close your eyes for a few seconds, open them, and see how far the band reached. Put the marker between your open level and that.

Sets the marker for you. When tracking starts and every 5 minutes after, if your face is in view, it reads your eyes up to 30 times a second for 30 seconds. From your blinks and rests it works out the range your eyes read when open and when closed (the two thin bands above the bar), and puts the marker halfway between them. Moving the marker yourself turns this off.

Saves battery when you're away: after 5 minutes with no face in view, the camera and the face and body models switch off and the screen may sleep. Every 10 minutes the camera comes on for 10 seconds to look for you, until it finds you. Moving the mouse, pressing a key or coming back to this window looks straight away.

The noise keeps playing the whole time. With your eyes closed the filter follows your breath; with them open, or when your breath isn't being tracked, it glides down to the low cutoff and rests there.

After 5 minutes in view of the camera without closing your eyes for 5 seconds, the noise’s pitch starts rising slowly from the low cutoff, doubling every 3 minutes, until you close your eyes for 5 seconds.
