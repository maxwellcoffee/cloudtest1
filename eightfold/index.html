Eightfold

Say something and hear it back, looped, layered with what you said before. A quiet tick marks the start of each cycle. Use headphones: the mic is always listening.

Press Start

Each thing you say becomes a loop that layers on top of the others.

How much of what you say becomes each loop, and the length of the cycle everything plays to, from 0.01 to 10.00 seconds.

s

Time from when you start speaking to when that loop first plays, from 0.01 to 2.00 seconds. While other loops are playing, a new one waits for the next cycle so everything stays in time with the tick.

×

How many cycles each loop stays for. 8 is the standard.

×

The most loops that play at the same moment. Below Times to loop, loops take turns, some playing every second or third cycle. Above it, the extra room goes to some loops playing again from the middle of the cycle.

%

The tick at the start of each cycle. Very short cycles tick at most five times a second.

Turns on the browser's echo cancelling so the loops are less likely to record themselves. It helps, but headphones work better.

Space start or stop · Esc clear all loops Keep it as a Mac app: in Safari choose File › Add to Dock. In Chrome choose Install from the menu. Your voice stays on this computer. Each loop is held in memory while it plays; nothing is saved or uploaded.
